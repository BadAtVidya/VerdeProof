# VerdeProof Brand Brief

## Tagline

Prove what matters. Keep what’s yours.

## Key messages

1. Buyers get a verifiable compliance answer without collecting a supplier’s full confidential record.
2. Labs issue signed evidence once; suppliers reuse private proofs across buyer requirements.
3. Midnight makes the public result auditable while the raw measurement stays private.

## Palette

- Forest: `#163D32`
- Paper: `#F4F5ED`
- Lime accent: `#C6F07A`
- Teal: `#49B58D`
- Ink: `#183029`

## X bio

Private compliance proofs for supply chains. Prove the threshold. Keep the raw evidence. Built on Midnight.

## Banner concept

Deep forest background. A fine lime proof line travels from a lab flask to a verified checkmark, with the measurement redacted between them. Keep the wordmark small and leave clear space for profile UI.
